# Dear-Data-100

1. Project Topic:
   A study of my phone usage during university lectures.

2. Research Question:
   How often do I check my phone during university lectures, and what patterns can I identify in my phone-checking habits?
   
3. Why I chose this topic?
   I chose this topic because checking my phone is something that happens in everyday life, including during university lectures. I am interested in finding out how often I actually check my phone and whether I do it more frequently than I realise.

Sometimes checking my phone can be an automatic habit, and I might not notice how often I do it. By collecting data over time, I will be able to reflect on my behaviour and identify patterns that I might not have noticed before.

I also chose this topic because it is personal and relatable. Many university students use their phones throughout the day, so the project could help me understand my own habits while exploring a wider issue related to attention and technology.
